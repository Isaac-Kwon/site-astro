## Current

### DarkSide Experiment
*Università degli Studi di Torino (UniTo), Ph.D., 2025 – present*

DarkSide is a direct dark-matter search experiment using a liquid-argon time projection chamber at the Laboratori Nazionali del Gran Sasso (LNGS), Italy.

Research topic: *Frontier Readout Technologies for Liquid Detectors*.

## Past

### CMS Experiment
*Natural Science Research Institute (NSRI), University of Seoul, 2022 – 2025*

[CMS](https://cms.cern) (Compact Muon Solenoid) is a general-purpose detector at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) at [CERN](https://home.cern).

### ePIC Barrel Imaging Calorimeter (BIC)
*Natural Science Research Institute (NSRI), University of Seoul + koBIC Collaboration, 2025*

The Barrel Imaging Calorimeter is the barrel electromagnetic calorimeter of [ePIC](https://www.epic-eic.org), the first detector at the [Electron-Ion Collider (EIC)](https://www.bnl.gov/eic/).

### Neutron Detector using GEM (Gas Electron Multiplier)
*University of Seoul, 2022 – 2025 · supported by NRF*

Boron-10 coated GEM detector for (cold) neutron detection and imaging, with Geant4 studies of the detector structure.
See [Publications](/publications/).

### Dual Readout Calorimeter
*University of Seoul + DRC Collaboration, 2023 – 2024*

Quality measurement of scintillating fibres.

### Radiation-Shielding Window Glass
*University of Seoul, 2024 – 2025 · supported by NRF, with KIST, KIMS and KAERI*

All-in-one window glass for radiation shielding, bullet-proofing and thermal insulation, and simulation of radiation shielding for survivability in space.

### OpenKIAS "Science with Citizen" — muonpi
*University of Seoul, with KIAS, 2023 – 2025*

A citizen-science cosmic-muon detector project.
[GitHub](https://github.com/muonpi-kr) · [Blog](https://muonpi.tistory.com)

### Heavy Ion Physics Experiment Laboratory (HIPEx)

The **Heavy Ion Physics Experiment Laboratory** is a research group in the Department of Physics at Pusan National University, Busan, Republic of Korea.
More details are on the [lab website](http://hipex.phys.pusan.ac.kr) (Korean).

### ALICE Collaboration

[ALICE](https://alice.cern) (A Large Ion Collider Experiment) is the heavy-ion experiment at the Large Hadron Collider (LHC) at [CERN](https://home.cern).
It studies strongly interacting matter at extreme energy densities, such as the quark–gluon plasma.

Contributions:

- **ITS upgrade**: Mass Chip Test (MCT) and Outer Barrel Hybrid Integrated Circuit (OB-HIC) assembly at Pusan National University (2017 – 2019)
- **CERN Summer Student 2019**: analysis of cosmic-ray data taken during the commissioning of the new ALICE ITS ([report](http://cds.cern.ch/record/2687398))
- **ITS operation**: ALICE-ITS on-call (2021 – 2022)

More: [ALICE Collaboration](https://alice-collaboration.web.cern.ch)
